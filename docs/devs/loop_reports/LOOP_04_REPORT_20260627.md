## 5. 연구 및 근본원인 분석

- `11698897` root cause: `랫따싶` tag appears on more than one NovelPia official detail. The previous scorer rewarded shared alias/tag evidence and generic OCR tokens enough that unrelated official detail `406113` outranked the exact bracketed `[림월드]` detail `382324`.
- Generalized fix: do not hardcode the post or title. Prefer an official detail only when its bracket cue is also present in post-side text/OCR/work cues, and avoid generic OCR-token boosts.
- Remaining precision leak: `11661857` shows a separate generic phrase/context-fiction problem. The user-invented scenario `로판속 아카데미 앞 두쫀쿠 가게 사장` can still be interpreted as an official Academy work when search evidence is noisy.
- Remaining recall gap: `11694019`, `11700470`, `11685994`, and `11663521` need source-specific character/OCR/domain bridges rather than broad public title promotion.
- Trade-off: tightening generic promotion improves negative precision, but positive recall still needs narrow source bridges. This loop improved positive pass rate from `1/8` to `3/8` without changing negative pass rate, but not enough for acceptance.
