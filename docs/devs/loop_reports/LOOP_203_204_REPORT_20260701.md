## 3) 연구 및 근본원인 분석

Root cause was not another title-specific rescue. The gap was a ledger propagation gap:

- `agentic_pipeline.py` could produce a final public title without a shared promotion-gate record when the title was already resolved by the ordinary final identity path.
- `candidate_audit.py` preserved `promotion_decision`, but did not preserve `candidate_ledger.promotion_gate_state/evidence` unless the same payload also existed top-level.
- `_sync_final_publication_ledger_contract(...)` then had to infer a safe-publication decision from serialized identity.

The fix keeps the architecture additive: resolved public titles are recorded through the same `GroundingState` / `ScopeState` / `PromotionDecision` contract, and candidate audit preserves the gate state rather than re-inventing it.
